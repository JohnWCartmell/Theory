categories with supports and cosupports as categories of binary relationships. Writing such categories being saturated (word out of the blue) which is when every binary relationship is a zigzag of deterministic morphisms. \item just getting a paper circulated and on research gate  so that I have shared these ideas.
\item between these two extremes it is possible to restructure this single paper so that the substance is described and an overview proof is given with detail relegated to later sections.
Currently the tightening and shortening sections are likely to dishearten the reader.

\begin{aside}
I must user the term zigzag throughout and say that in an RR.5 range category every zigzag has a normal form. These normal forms are the 
short sticklebacks described by Cockett. 

It is potentially confusing though because these normal forms are tuples of morphisms 
of \catcw and these morphisms are generated from the sketch. The morphisms themselves expressed as paths of arrows from the sketch do not in general have normal forms.

$shorten(T(F))$ can be potentially written as $normalised(F)$.
\end{aside}
